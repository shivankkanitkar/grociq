Questions, answered

The short version.

Do I have to manually add every grocery?

Initially, yes. Future versions may explore receipt scanning and order-history integrations.

How does GrocIQ know when something will run out?

It learns from purchase frequency and consumption patterns, then surfaces a prediction for you to review.

Can I order groceries through GrocIQ?

Not currently. Grocery-delivery integrations are part of the future vision.

Is GrocIQ only for budgeting?

No. GrocIQ combines grocery tracking, predictive refills, recurring purchases, Smart Basket recommendations, spending insights and nutrition tracking.
